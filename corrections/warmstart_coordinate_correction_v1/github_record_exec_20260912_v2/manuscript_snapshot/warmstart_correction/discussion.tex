The coordinate-corrected results support lower objectives relative to
cold starts on the fixed signal-enriched Max-3SAT subset. They do not
establish uniform improvement across families or the original E4 sample,
nor a clear additional benefit of pair moments over the independence
closure. Intervals containing zero indicate uncertain evidence, not
equivalence or absence of any effect. The small original spin-glass
active-auxiliary subset ($n=2$) yields particularly wide intervals.
All contrasts are exploratory and unadjusted for multiplicity.
