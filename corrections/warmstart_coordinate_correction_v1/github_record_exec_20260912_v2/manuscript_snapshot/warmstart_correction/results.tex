\paragraph{Frozen correction study.}
We reran all 480 targeted tasks on the original 20 selected instances and
completed all 4,320 original E4 tasks on its original 14 instances.
Archived marginals, representations, seeds, and budgets were fixed.
The execution comprises 4,332 fresh optimizations and 468 validated
zero-layer initial-state records. Each fresh restart retains the original
60-evaluation COBYLA budget and early-stop accounting rule. Zero-layer
records are not effective optimizations. We average three restarts, then
five matched draws where applicable, before pairing policies within raw
instances. Families, budgets, and studies are separate; intervals are
unadjusted exploratory Student-t intervals with $n-1$ degrees of freedom.

\paragraph{Targeted findings.}
On the fixed signal-enriched Max-3SAT subset ($n=10$), pair-minus-cold
differences are $-6.7198$ [$-9.2712,-4.1683$] at $p=1$ and $-6.7971$
[$-9.1395,-4.4547$] at $p=2$. Both spin-glass intervals include zero.
Original-only and independence starts also improve over cold on this
Max-3SAT subset. Pair-minus-original-only at $p=2$ is $-0.4759$
[$-0.8454,-0.1063$], while its $p=1$ interval includes zero.
Neither pair-minus-independence interval excludes zero.

\paragraph{Original E4 findings.}
The primary selected active-auxiliary subset retains two spin-glass and
seven Max-3SAT instances. All eight pair-minus-cold intervals include zero.
For Max-3SAT, the mean differences at $p=1$, $p=2$, $B=128$, and $B=256$
are $-0.8717$, $-0.8731$, $-0.6768$, and $-0.6218$, respectively.
At $B=128$, both selected spin-glass instances and two Max-3SAT instances
have zero layers; they remain labelled in the all-scheduled summary.
Positive-depth and zero-depth diagnostics are also reported separately.
The Max-3SAT pair-minus-original-only contrast at $p=2$ is $-0.3108$
[$-0.5562,-0.0654$]; this secondary exploratory contrast does not establish
the primary pair-versus-cold claim.
