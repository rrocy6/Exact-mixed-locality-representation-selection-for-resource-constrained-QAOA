`corrections/review_v2_20260920/main_repaired.tex` from the accepted R12
delivery. The original `paper/main.tex` in that delivery remains in the local
archive; the change from it is limited to the audited five-seed retention
wording, family/topology table order, artifact paths, and stability and
selector interpretation. This PR presents the integrated manuscript source
for review. Final adoption and publication remain decisions for the authors.
